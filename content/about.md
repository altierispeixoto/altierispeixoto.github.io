I'm **Altieris Peixoto**, an AI leader based in **Auckland, New Zealand**. I work at the intersection of machine learning, engineering, and product, building systems that need to actually work at scale.

I lead the Data Science & AI team at [Auror](https://www.auror.co/), a retail crime intelligence platform used by 100+ retailers across 6 countries. The team builds the ML systems that power how retailers detect, investigate, and respond to crime.

## What I Work On

I manage a small team of ML engineers and data scientists, and I'm responsible for our technical direction across the full stack of data and AI. Most of what we build sits at the intersection of computer vision, entity resolution, complex networks and large-scale data.

Some of the systems I've led from research to production:

- **Entity Resolution**: connecting fragmented crime records across stores and systems to build coherent identities
- **Face Matching**: linking incidents across locations to identify repeat offenders
- **Fairness & Bias Auditing**: evaluating bias in third-party face recognition providers before we rely on them
- **Voice Event Report**: a voice AI interface that lets retail staff report incidents hands-free
- **Organized Retail Crime Detection**: surfacing coordinated theft patterns across retail networks

Outside of Auror, I'm the track lead for **AI, Data & Ethics** at [NZ Tech Rally 2026](https://nztechrally.nz/). I'm also part of the OB Program 2026 as a Board Observer, getting exposure to startup governance in the Auckland ecosystem.

## Background

I have an **M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering** from the Federal University of Technology – Paraná (UTFPR), Brazil. My research used Time Varying Graph Networks to optimize public transport in Curitiba, applying graph theory and optimization methods. That work led to publications across peer-reviewed journals and conferences and you can find them on [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Altieris-Peixoto).
